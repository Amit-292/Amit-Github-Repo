## THE SOLUTION (How it works)

```
Customer scans QR
        ↓
Browses menu (instant, no app)
        ↓
Adds items to cart
        ↓
Submits order
        ↓
ADMIN REVIEWS (prevents mistakes) ← KEY DIFFERENCE
        ↓
Kitchen sees approved order (live)
        ↓
Kitchen updates status: Pending → Preparing → Ready
        ↓
Customer gets notification
        ↓
Bill auto-generated (with 5% GST)
        ↓
Customer pays via UPI QR (30 secs) or WhatsApp
        ↓
Done! Bill moved to history
```

**Why Admin Approval Gate?** Prevents kitchen mistakes. Reduces waste. Improves quality.
