## By Eksperty Pvt Ltd

## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

**RestroIQ** is a cloud-based restaurant billing and kitchen management system designed specifically for the Indian restaurant market. It solves critical operational problems for small-to-medium restaurants by automating billing, streamlining kitchen workflows, and providing real-time business insights.

**Key Position**: "Simple. Fast. Affordable. Restaurant Management That Just Works"

**Target Market**: Small-medium casual restaurants (50-200 seats), cafés, fast-food chains, and cloud kitchens across India.

**Pricing**: ₹499-₹1,999/month (SaaS) or ₹49,999 (On-Premise License)

**Timeline to Profitability**: 4-6 weeks (ROI through error reduction and efficiency gains)

---

## 1. PRODUCT POSITIONING

### Core Value Proposition
RestroIQ eliminates manual billing chaos and kitchen confusion by creating a seamless digital ordering, approval, and payment system that restaurants can implement in 1 day.

### Unique Selling Points
1. **Group Billing (Unique)** - Split bills for groups (up to 8 per table). Only major feature competitors lack.
2. **Zero Setup Friction** - No app needed. Customers scan QR → Instant menu. Works on any phone.
3. **Order Approval Gate** - Admin reviews before kitchen cooks. Prevents mistakes and waste.
4. **Built for India** - GST (5%), UPI payments, WhatsApp integration, Indian support team.
5. **Fast Deployment** - Live in 1 day. No hardware needed.

### Market Category
**"QR-based POS for Small Restaurants"** 

This is a growing category in India as restaurants move from manual systems to tech-enabled billing. Similar to: DineWizz, RestroApp, Waiter.in (but at lower price point and faster implementation).

## 3. KEY PROBLEMS SOLVED

### Problem 1: Manual Billing Delays ❌ → ✅ Instant Digital Bills
- **Before**: Customers wait 10-15 minutes at counter for handwritten bills
- **After**: Bill generated in 30 seconds. Customers can pay from table.
- **Impact**: Reduced wait times, better customer experience, higher turnover

### Problem 2: Billing Errors ❌ → ✅ 100% Accurate, GST-Compliant
- **Before**: 5% of bills have calculation errors. GST filing is manual.
- **After**: All amounts automated. GST calculated correctly every time.
- **Impact**: Recovered ₹5,000-10,000/month in billing discrepancies. Stress-free tax filing.

### Problem 3: Kitchen Chaos ❌ → ✅ Real-Time Order Management
- **Before**: No visibility of incoming orders. Duplicates placed. Wrong quantities cooked.
- **After**: Live Kanban board. Admin review gate. Kitchen sees only approved orders.
- **Impact**: Reduced food waste. Faster food prep. Better quality. Happy customers.

### Problem 4: No Business Insights ❌ → ✅ Instant Reports & Analytics
- **Before**: Owner has no idea which items sell. Can't make data-driven menu decisions.
- **After**: Best sellers report. Daily revenue tracking. Customer feedback ratings.
- **Impact**: Optimize menu. Remove slow-moving items. Maximize profit margin.

### Problem 5: Inventory Loss ❌ → ✅ Better Tracking (Coming Soon)
- **Before**: No tracking of what's used vs sold. Theft goes undetected.
- **After**: (Coming Q1 2025) Inventory management with consumption tracking.
- **Impact**: Catch losses early. Reduce shrinkage by 5-10%.

### Problem 6: Manual Reconciliation ❌ → ✅ Automated End-of-Day Reports
- **Before**: 30+ minutes spent manually counting bills and reconciling cash.
- **After**: Reports generated in seconds. Ready for accounting/GST filing.
- **Impact**: Save 2 hours/day for staff. Reduce human error in accounting.

## 7. IDEAL CUSTOMER USE CASES

### Use Case 1: Casual Restaurant (60 seats)
- **Current Pain**: Manual billing, long queues, billing errors
- **Solution**:
  - Customers scan QR at table → Browse menu on phone → Order sent to kitchen
  - Admin approves orders → Kitchen sees live Kanban
  - Bill auto-generated → Customers pay via UPI QR
  - End-of-day: Full reports in 2 minutes
- **Benefit**: 40% reduction in billing time. Zero errors. Better customer satisfaction.
- **Price**: Professional plan (₹999/month) for 20-25 tables

### Use Case 2: Café Chain (3 locations, 40 seats each)
- **Current Pain**: Inconsistent operations across outlets. No unified data.
- **Solution**:
  - Each outlet sets up separately (1 day each)
  - Headquarters gets daily email reports
  - Best sellers consolidated across locations
  - Centralized menu management (future: multi-location)
- **Benefit**: Consistency. Unified insights. Better decision-making.
- **Price**: Business plan (₹1,999/month) with priority support

### Use Case 3: Cloud Kitchen (Delivery-only)
- **Current Pain**: Manual order management. No real-time tracking. Long delivery times.
- **Solution**:
  - Orders come in through aggregators
  - Staff create "virtual tables" for each order
  - Kitchen sees live status
  - Delivery partner notified when order is ready
- **Benefit**: Faster execution. Better delivery partner coordination.
- **Price**: Professional or Business plan depending on volume

### Use Case 4: Small QSR (Counter service, 100+ daily orders)
- **Current Pain**: High-speed billing required. Staff training difficult. Errors compound.
- **Solution**:
  - Customers at counter order via kiosk/tablet
  - Billing automated instantly
  - Kitchen gets clear, mistake-free orders
  - Peak hours handled smoothly
- **Benefit**: 3x faster billing. Reduced training overhead. Happy customers.
- **Price**: Starter or Professional plan (₹499-₹999/month)

## 18. FAQ FOR SALES TEAM

**Q: Why should a restaurant choose RestroIQ over manual billing?**
A: Simple. Restaurants save ₹28,000/month on average through error reduction, staff efficiency, and reduced waste. It pays for itself in less than 1 day.

**Q: What if customers don't have smartphones?**
A: Rare in 2025, but you can also print QR codes on table tents. Tablets work too.

**Q: Can we integrate with Swiggy/Zomato?**
A: Currently orders must be manually entered. Integration coming in Q2 2025.

**Q: What if internet goes down?**
A: Kitchen display will pause, but UPI payment works offline with camera scan. On-premise solution handles this better.

**Q: How is this different from Dinewave or other POS systems?**
A: We're lighter (no hardware), faster to deploy (1 day), and cheaper (₹499 vs ₹2,000+). Perfect for small restaurants.
