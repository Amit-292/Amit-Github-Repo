Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Do customers need to download an app?

No! Customers scan QR code → Browser opens menu. Works on any phone. No app needed. This is a key advantage.

Q: What if internet goes down?

Kitchen display will freeze but UPI cameras work offline. For on-premise deployments, the system continues working locally.

Q: Can I customize the menu categories?

Yes! Categories are fully customizable (Starters, Main Course, etc.). Add/remove as many as you want.

Q: Is GST rate fixed at 5%?

Currently yes (standard 5% for food in India). Custom GST rates coming in Q1 2025 for Business plan.

Q: How many tables can I add?

Starter: 10 tables | Professional: 50 tables | Business: Unlimited. Easy to upgrade anytime.

Q: Can I see historical reports?

Yes! Full bill history with date filtering, best sellers report, and customer feedback analytics.

Q: What payment methods do you accept?

Subscription: Credit card, Debit card, Net Banking via Razorpay. Payments from customers: UPI (PhonePe, Google Pay, BHIM, Paytam).

Q: Can I export billing data?

Yes! Export bills as PDF, feedback as Excel/PDF. Perfect for accounting and GST filing.

Q: Is training provided?

Absolutely! Video tutorials, live demo sessions, and written guides included with every plan.

Q: Can I switch plans later?

Yes! Upgrade/downgrade anytime. Prorated billing ensures fair pricing. No lock-in contracts.
